# Market data and point-in-time signal

## Snapshot

The Lab 20 Yahoo Finance chart responses for VOO, SPY, BND, VB and BIL were retrieved October 22, 2026. [Source requests, fields, retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 fingerprints](../data/sources/provenance.json) identify the locally retained vendor responses. The locally retained aligned price table (`data/prices.csv`) covers September 2021 through September 2026. Raw timestamps are converted to exchange-local dates. For each completed calendar month, the last available adjusted close with a valid observation is retained. The engine re-aggregates the raw responses, checks their hashes and reconciles every month to the bundled CSV. The CSV includes USD adjusted prices used to calculate monthly adjusted-close total-return proxies. There is no imputation or cross-asset forward fill. The first price month has no return. The engine rejects gaps or nonpositive prices.

The adjusted-close data comes from one later vendor snapshot. It does not establish what each historical data vintage contained and corrections may affect estimates and realized returns. The fixed ETF universe is a retrospective classroom choice and does not represent a historical universe selection exercise. The snapshot contains no client identifiers or credentials.

## Reviewer access and reproduction

Yahoo’s U.S. terms require prior permission for automated data collection and restrict service and data uses. Redistribution permission for the historical archive has not been established. Keep the raw responses and monthly price CSV outside the public repository. The app does not call Yahoo, and a private repository is not a substitute for permission. [Yahoo U.S. Terms of Service](https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/terms/otos/index.html?lang=en-US)

The public checkout uses deterministic synthetic inputs generated by the project. These exercise the same portfolio engine, constraints, stress controls and UI without vendor data. Historical evidence is recorded in the executed JSON results. Exact historical replay is conditional on the reviewer having authorized access to the original vintage. Put `prices.csv` under `data/` and the five original ticker JSON files under `data/sources/` beside `provenance.json`. The engine checks the raw SHA-256 hashes and all aligned monthly prices. It fails on a mismatched archive. A later snapshot may yield different results. This package supplies no automatic collection route and no assurance that a reviewer can obtain that original vintage.

## Signal card

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Name | SPY six-month price momentum |
| Field | Yahoo Finance chart `indicators.quote[0].close` in USD, sampled at the last available session close within each month. |
| Definition | (P_t / P_{t-6} - 1), where (t) is the calendar month before execution. Uses price closes for a price trend. Estimated and realized total returns use adjusted closes. |
| Availability | A close is available after its session closes. The engine uses data through September for each October rebalance. |
| Use | Subtract 2 annual percentage points from both VOO and SPY arithmetic return inputs when momentum is negative. A nonnegative reading leaves both inputs unchanged. The threshold and penalty are implementation assumptions. |
| Exclusions and revisions | Missing or nonpositive monthly closes stop the calculation. The bundled SPY payload contains no split events. A split event would require review before running this rule. The archived file provides a reproducible proxy for historically observable closes. Vendor corrections are possible. |
| Evidence | Both observed cutoffs had positive momentum. The sample provides no realized evidence about decisions under the negative-signal branch. |

## Modeling conventions

- Estimates use monthly arithmetic mean × 12 and sample covariance × 12. The risk cap is the benchmark weight vector applied to that same covariance. Optional shrinkage reduces estimated cross-sleeve covariances while retaining the VOO/SPY covariance block. Annualized estimated return and realized compound return measure different quantities.
- Close-to-close evaluation uses the October month-end close as an execution proxy. Prices for drifting existing holdings to pretrade weights are observed at the rebalance close. The preceding month sets the signal and estimates. Actual fill prices and time to trade may differ.
- Start every strategy and the benchmark with a $100,000 BIL position. Trading cost is the rate in basis points multiplied by one-way turnover, defined as half the sum of absolute target-minus-pretrade weights. Expected net return is `(1 − cost fraction) × (1 + modeled gross return) − 1`. The simulation deducts the same cost fraction from pretrade NAV and invests the remaining NAV at target weights. Fund expenses are reflected in observed fund returns. Separate taxes, advisory fees and market impact are excluded. The benchmark pays the same modeled cost.
- The optimizer searches all fully invested, long-only portfolios in five-percentage-point steps. It minimizes volatility among grid portfolios that pass the structural, risk and net-return tests. An infeasible result applies to this grid. The committee can review grid resolution before treating a narrow miss as economically conclusive.
- BIL is the qualifying Treasury-bill reserve proxy. All approved ETFs are modeled as sellable within five business days under ordinary conditions. This is an assumption about exchange liquidity rather than a test of market depth or stressed execution. Direct issuer concentration is zero in this universe because the mandate exempts diversified funds. No look-through issuer limits are assumed. Annual schedule and target compliance are evaluated at rebalances. Holdings can drift between them.
- VOO and SPY both target the S&P 500. Their combined weight is the large-cap sleeve reported by the app. Their shared covariance captures overlap in the risk estimate. Equal weighting puts 40% in the same index exposure through two wrappers. Smaller differences in fees, trading and tracking can still affect which wrapper the optimizer chooses.
